Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This policy explains how Brian Baker, the publisher of Senate Escape, handles information in the game and on this support website.

The short version

Your game progress and settings stay on your device. There is no game account, purchase system, or publisher-operated gameplay analytics service. Advertising services and Apple’s TestFlight can process data as explained below.

Progress and settings

The game saves unlocked levels, completed levels, best times, and preferences such as sound locally on your device or browser. The game does not upload these records to a publisher-operated server or offer account-based cloud sync.

Local records remain until you erase the app’s data or browser site data. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore saved information.

Advertising

Native iOS builds with advertising use Google AdMob. Public releases use Senate Escape’s own ad units. Internal test builds may use Google’s test ads. Banner ads are limited to menus and result screens. Rewarded ads are optional and can provide an in-game coffee charge; watching an ad is not required to play.

Ad requests are configured as non-personalized. This does not mean that no information is processed. Google’s services may receive your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app or device identifiers, advertising and interaction information, and crash or performance diagnostics. These can support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service reliability.

See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google’s Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google’s own policies govern its data handling and retention.

Your advertising choices

The app uses Google’s consent tools before requesting ads. Where a privacy message is required, the app presents it and checks whether ads may be requested. Where available, the app’s ad-privacy control lets you review or change those choices.

This build does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized ads can still use identifiers for purposes such as limiting repeated ads and reporting, subject to applicable consent choices. Google explains non-personalized ads here.

You may decline an optional rewarded ad and continue playing. If an ad is unavailable, skipped, or does not report an earned reward, no coffee reward is granted.

TestFlight beta testing

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares it with the app’s publisher. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit may also be shared. Whether the publisher receives your name or email depends on how you joined the test. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the game.

Apple manages the TestFlight service and its retention rules. Read TestFlight & Privacy or visit Apple’s Data and Privacy page for Apple-related choices.

Support messages and requests

If you email senateescape@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to answer you and troubleshoot the issue, and retain correspondence only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. We do not use support messages for advertising.

Contact us at the same address with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information held by the publisher. Rights and available choices may depend on your location. Local game records can be removed on your device; information held by Google or Apple is also subject to their own controls and policies.

This website and policy updates

This website contains no analytics script, advertising SDK, or contact form. Its hosting provider may process ordinary connection and security logs to deliver and protect the site.

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions can be sent to senateescape@gmail.com.
