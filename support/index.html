Controls and objectives

Help the fictional senator evade reporters and press photographers seeking a photo or comment. Stay out of their camera cones and reach the exit.

Use the on-screen thumbstick to steer the wheelchair. Boost gives a short burst of speed; Distract offers the press free donuts. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to boost, and press Space to distract. Find a coffee and use Q or the coffee button for a Coffee rush.

Each level shows its objective before you begin. Pick up the marked objects, visit stops in order where instructed, and pause at a marked desk, reader, or outbox to complete its action. Finish the objective to open the exit. Home, retry, and the next level are available through the game’s menus.

Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS version, the game version or TestFlight build number, and which level you were playing. A short description of what you did and what happened helps us reproduce it. A screenshot or screen recording is useful when you have one. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

For a TestFlight beta, you can also send feedback through TestFlight. Its privacy information explains what accompanies that feedback.

Report an ad

To report an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad, email senateescape@gmail.com. Include the advertiser or product name, approximate time, and what concerned you. A screenshot is helpful if you can safely capture one. Please do not send personal or payment information. You can also use the ad’s own information or reporting control when available.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved on this device. There is no game account or account-based cloud sync. Deleting the app or clearing browser site data may erase your progress.

Do I need to watch ads to play?

No. Rewarded ads are optional. In native builds with ads, a completed rewarded ad may grant one coffee charge. Skipped, failed, or unavailable ads do not grant a reward, and you can continue without one.

Why is there no ad available?

Ad availability can depend on your connection, privacy choices, and the ad service. Internal test builds may use test ads. If an ad does not load, you can keep playing.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Use the app’s ad-privacy control where it is available. You can also read the privacy policy and contact support with questions.

How do I report a missing coffee reward?

Tell us the level, build number, and what happened after the ad closed. The game only grants a reward when the ad service reports that it was earned.
